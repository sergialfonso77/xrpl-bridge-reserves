    What this does and does not show

    • Backing is the gateway's XRP balance minus the reserve the XRPL itself locks. Only validated ledgers are used.
    • Outstanding is summed over every chain where the bridged-XRP token exists, enumerated from the Axelar ITS Hub, so a new destination cannot be left out silently (the report turns unverified instead).
    • On XRPL EVM it is the native supply; on Hedera and Stellar it is the supply tracked by the ITS Hub, which caps what can be redeemed. Stellar has no independent on-chain check yet.
    • The surplus is expected: gas fees paid on deposits stay in the gateway, as do deposits with unusable gas memos and some direct funding.
    • Measured outstanding is about 0.03% above what XRPL history predicts. That is the safe direction (liabilities overstated) but it is not yet fully explained.
    • Read-only monitor. Not an audit and not financial advice.
